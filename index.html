Who's Writing This

Atul Masih

Slow traveler, based in Lucknow

I don't travel like a tourist ticking off a list. I travel the way you'd revisit a place you already love — slowly, and paying attention to the small things everyone else walks past.

Travelheart started because I got tired of guides that tell you where to stand for a photo but nothing about why a place actually matters. What I write instead is closer to a conversation with the ghats, the old lanes, the people who've lived somewhere for sixty years and have opinions about it.

Travel Less. Feel More. isn't a slogan I picked for the homepage — it's the whole point. If you've ever come home from a trip more exhausted than when you left, this journal is for you.
